A LITTLE ABOUT ME

Curious eyes.
Intentional frames.

I'm Darshan Wakh, a video editor and cinematographer drawn to the art of visual storytelling. From the first idea to the final cut, I enjoy shaping images, movement and sound into stories that connect with people.

My work spans commercial films, automobiles, social media, music videos, travel and documentary-style content. I believe every project deserves its own visual language.
